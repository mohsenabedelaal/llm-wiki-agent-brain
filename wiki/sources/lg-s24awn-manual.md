## Control Setpoints & Operation Rules

- **Temperature Setpoints**: Cooling range 18°C to 30°C; Heating range 16°C to 30°C.
- **Fan Speed Steps**: Low, Medium, High, CHAOS (natural breeze logic).
- **Auto Operation / Auto Changeover**: Automatically switches cooling/heating to maintain set temperature within ±2°C.
- **Healthy Dehumidification**: Automatically controls room temperature and airflow based on ambient humidity/temperature algorithm (user temperature setting disabled).
- **Jet Cool / Jet Heat Mode**:
  - Jet Cool: Super high fan speed at 18°C setpoint for 30 minutes.
  - Jet Heat: Super high fan speed at 30°C setpoint for 60 minutes.
- **NEO PLASMA Purification**: Generates high-charge electron plasma to kill contaminants.
- **Auto Clean Function**: Runs indoor fan for ~30 minutes with closed louvers post-shutdown to dry internal coil moisture.
- **Sleep Mode**: Increases cooling setpoint by 1°C per 30 minutes twice (+2°C total over 1 hour) with slow fan speed. Timer selectable 1 to 7 hours.
- **Test Operation**: Hold manual ON/OFF button 3–5 seconds (1 beep). Forces cooling at high fan speed for 18 minutes regardless of room temperature.
- **Auto Restart**: Restores last operating parameters after power restoration. Toggle on/off by holding ON/OFF button for 6 seconds (beeps twice, indicator blinks 4 times).

(source: S24AWN.pdf)

## Electrical & Maintenance Parameters

- **Electrical Requirements**: Dedicated circuit and breaker; proper grounding required.
- **Extension Cord Specification**: Temporary use only with CSA certified / UL listed 3-wire grounded cord rated **15 A, 125 V**.
- **Air Filter Cleaning**: Clean every 2 weeks using vacuum or lukewarm water (below 40°C).
- **Plasma Filter Cleaning**: Clean every 3 months.
- **Deodorizer Filters**: Re-activate in direct sunlight for 2 hours.
- **High-Voltage Warning**: Do not touch plasma filter within 10 seconds of opening the inlet grille to prevent electric shock.
- **Compressor Protection**: 3-minute restart delay after power outage.

(source: S24AWN.pdf)

## Self-Diagnosis LED Codes

- **Thermistor Error**: LED blinks 1 time per 3-second cycle indicates open or short circuit on indoor room temperature thermistor or indoor pipe temperature thermistor.

(source: S24AWN.pdf)
