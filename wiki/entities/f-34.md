# F-34

**Summary**: Combination fire/smoke damper located on the third-floor supply air trunk serving [[ahu-3]].

## Operational details & interlocks

- **Power feed**: [[lp-3]] Circuit 13 (20A, 1-pole, 277V actuator/status power). (source: daep-electrical-panel-schedule.md)
- **Safety Interlock**: F-34 must prove fully open before [[ahu-3]] supply fan enable is granted by the BAS. (source: daep-ahu-sequence-of-operation.md)
- **Commissioning Status**: Proved open during functional testing on 2019-08-22. (source: daep-ahu-commissioning-report-2019.md)
- **Diagnostic Role**: A partially closed or stuck F-34 damper restricts duct airflow, causing high static pressure and elevated fan power, making it a key check for [[fan-pwr-high]]. (source: hvac-fault-library-fan-power-high.md)
